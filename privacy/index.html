Overview

Marcut is a native Mac app that redacts sensitive information from documents. It processes files entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud processing, and no transmission of document content.

Data We Collect

We do not collect or store your document content. Files you open or drag into Marcut stay on your device.

  • Document content: processed locally only.
  • Account data: none (no account required).
  • Analytics/ads: none.

If you download an optional local AI model, the download is fetched from a hosting server. The host may receive standard request metadata (such as IP address). No document content is ever transmitted.

How We Use Data

  • To process and redact documents locally on your Mac.
  • To store your app preferences and settings on your device.
  • To download optional local AI models when you choose to install them.

Processing remains on-device regardless of whether AI is enabled.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Marcut has no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics.

Apple may collect diagnostic or crash data if you have opted in to share it with developers through system settings. This data is handled under Apple’s privacy policy.

Local Storage and Retention

  • Redacted outputs are saved only to the location you choose.
  • Downloaded AI models are stored locally and can be deleted in Settings.
  • Temporary processing happens in memory and is not retained by the app.
  • If you enable debugging under settings, logs files may contain document content.

Your Choices

  • Control file access by choosing which files to open or drag into the app.
  • Use Rules-Only mode to avoid downloading any AI models.
  • Delete downloaded models at any time in Settings by clicking Reveal Models.
  • Use Clear Logs under settings to securely erase logs.

Redaction is a professional review task. You remain responsible for verifying outputs before distribution or filing.

Children’s Privacy

Marcut is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top indicates when it was last revised.
